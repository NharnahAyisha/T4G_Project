FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Answers to common questions about VitaBridge, our products, and how to reach us.

How do I create a VitaBridge account?

Tap "sign-up" in the top navigation, fill in your name, email, and password, and confirm you agree to the Terms & Condition. You'll be ready to log in right away.

What payment methods do you accept?

We currently accept Mobile Money (MoMo) to 054781601 (ISHA DEDEI ARMAH). Payment details are confirmed on the Cart page before checkout.

How long does delivery take?

Delivery within Accra typically takes 1-2 business days. For other regions in Ghana, please allow 3-5 business days.

Are your medical products genuine and safe?

Yes. Every product listed on VitaBridge is sourced from trusted, reputable suppliers and checked for quality before it's made available for purchase.

Can I get health advice directly from VitaBridge?

Our Topics section provides general health education content, but it isn't a substitute for professional medical advice. For personal health concerns, please consult a licensed healthcare provider.

How do I contact customer support?

Visit our Contact page to send a message, call us directly, or start a WhatsApp chat for urgent questions.
